What just happened

    THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE

    Every cab is an agent.

    The challenge is to serve riders without sending every empty cab to the same place. The DQN sees its own zone and adjacent zones, including open requests and idle neighbours. Cabs share learned network weights, while making decisions from local observations.

    1. ObserveRead local rider demand, nearby cabs, time and cab status.
    2. DecideChoose accept, wait, north, south, east or west using valid action scores.
    3. Serve & learnFares reward completed rides. Waiting, empty travel and unserved requests incur costs during training.

    Experiment setup & project scope

    5 × 5 grid; default fleet 8; 24-hour episodes; driver shift 06:00–22:00; pickup radius at most 3 zones; learned agents sense and accept within the pickup radius of 3 zones; cancellation of unmatched riders after 8 minutes; γ = 0.95; experience replay and target network; shared weights and a hybrid individual/city reward.

    This website replays saved Python executions of the trained checkpoint and baseline algorithms. Inference decisions and Q-values were computed during export. It does not train or run a neural network in your browser. All methods use the same replay seed, 810000. Changing a method or fleet loads that corresponding execution at the same time.

    Demand is synthetic, with morning/evening peaks and an airport hotspot. Full-day scores include overnight demand while drivers are off shift. Trips still pending at midnight are unserved. VDN is trained and benchmarked as an extension; QMIX, real taxi-data calibration, ride pooling, EV charging and pricing remain future work.
